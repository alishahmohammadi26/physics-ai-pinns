# Research Summaries

Short digests of the most influential works, each with contribution / advantages
/ limitations. For the full narrative see the
[literature review](../../references/Papers_and_Bibliography/literature_review_2017_2026.md).

## Must-read five
1. **Raissi, Perdikaris & Karniadakis (2019)** — the foundational PINN framework (forward + inverse). *[in repo]*
2. **Karniadakis et al. (2021)** — the field's definitive high-level overview. *[in repo]*
3. **Lu et al. (2021), DeepONet** — operator learning; generalise across inputs, infer in one forward pass.
4. **Wang, Sankaran, Wang & Perdikaris (2023)** — the practical "expert's guide" to actually training PINNs.
5. **Wang et al. (2024), PirateNets** — residual-adaptive networks that make very deep PINNs trainable. *[in repo]*

## By theme
- **Training/theory:** Krishnapriyan 2021 (failure modes); Wang 2022 (NTK); Wang 2022 (causality).
- **Sampling:** Wu 2023 (residual-based adaptive sampling).
- **Operators:** Li 2021 (FNO); Wang 2021 (physics-informed DeepONet); Cao 2024 (Laplace operator).
- **New architectures:** Liu 2024 (KAN); Wang 2025 (PIKAN); Shukla 2024 (fair MLP-vs-KAN comparison).
- **Recent reviews (2024–2026):** Farea 2024; Meng 2025; Toscano 2025; Fan & Chen 2026.
