PINN Foundations, 2019-2021
===========================

Contents
- pinn-foundations-2019-2021.html: long-form standalone blog article (figures embedded)
- PINN_Foundations_2019_2021.ipynb: executed PyTorch notebook
- PINN_literature_2019_2021.csv: curated 43-record literature map
- pinn_foundations_assets/: figures generated by the notebook

Scope
The literature window ends on 31 December 2021. The survey is a high-coverage,
curated technical map, not a guaranteed exhaustive bibliographic census. Several
publisher pages were available only through abstracts/metadata; those works are
summarized at the contribution level rather than reproduced as full-text reviews.

Validation
The notebook was executed top-to-bottom in Python 3 with no error outputs.
